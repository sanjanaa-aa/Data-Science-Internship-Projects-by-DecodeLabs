WEEK 2 - CREDIT DEFAULT PREDICTION

Dataset:
Default of Credit Card Clients Dataset (UCI Repository)

Model 1: Logistic Regression
--------------------------------
Precision : 0.3865
Recall    : 0.5666
ROC-AUC   : 0.6573

Model 2: Random Forest
--------------------------------
Precision : 0.5067
Recall    : 0.4890
ROC-AUC   : 0.6778

Final Comparison
--------------------------------
Logistic Regression ROC-AUC : 0.6573
Random Forest ROC-AUC       : 0.6778

Best Model: Random Forest

Conclusion:
Random Forest achieved the highest ROC-AUC score and better precision compared to Logistic Regression. Therefore, Random Forest was selected as the final model for predicting credit card payment defaults.
